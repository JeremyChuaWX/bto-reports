## 6. Summary

### Pros
- **Standard flat:** 5-year MOP, no clawback, whole-flat rental allowed after MOP [Official].
- **Large launch discount:** 4-room $339k–$445k against a ~$670k nearby young-flat median; 5-room $494k–$626k against ~$810k [Verified].
- **Pioneer Primary next door (187 m).** Kranji Primary (2028), Bukit View Primary (2027) and ACS(P) (2030) are all within about 1.5 km [Official / Estimate].
- **Three JRL stations within about 1 km straight-line:** JE2, JE1 and JS4. JS4 opens mid-2028, before keys [Verified].
- **Healthcare:** a dialysis centre on site, Tengah Polyclinic open, and the hospital due around 2031–32 [Official / Estimate].
- **Rare mix:** the only 3Gen flats in the exercise, and White flats up to 5-room [Official].
- **Future frontage** onto the park and waterbody [Official].

### Cons
- **No MRT walkable today.** Operating stations are 2.7–2.9 km on foot, and JE1/JE2 still have no confirmed date [Verified].
- **No hawker centre in Tengah.** The nearest is about a 1.7 km walk. The Town Centre has no date [Verified / Unverified].
- **The PIE severs walking links southward.** Jurong Primary, Jurongville Secondary and Jurong Polyclinic look close but are 2.4–3.2 km on foot [Verified].
- **Years of surrounding construction.** Keys are not expected until 3Q 2030; the backstop date is Mar 2032 [Official].
- **Tengah MOP supply overhang.** 3Gen resale demand is thin [Estimate].

### Who it suits
- First-timer families wanting a 4-room or 5-room flat in the west, especially those with children headed for Pioneer, Kranji or ACS(P).
- People working in Jurong Lake District, Jurong East, the Jurong Innovation District or NTU.
- Multi-generation households (only 28 3Gen units).
- Singles 35+ and seniors looking at 2-room Flexi from $131k.
- **Less suitable for:** daily CBD commuters, buyers who need keys before 2030, and buyers sensitive to noise who end up in a PIE-facing stack.

### Verdict [Estimate]
**Good value: one of the stronger Standard options in this exercise for 4-room and 5-room buyers.**
- **5-room odds are better than first thought:** it is the biggest allocation (252 units).
- **3Gen is the tight one** (28 units).
- **Stack choice:** prefer 140B or 141C for 5-room. Prefer stacks facing north, towards the school, or overlooking the park, rather than stacks facing the PIE. Check unit orientation on the portal.
