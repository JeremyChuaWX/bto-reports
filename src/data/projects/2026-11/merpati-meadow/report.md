**How the Plus rules hold back resale value** [Official, GC/MyNiceHome]
- 10-year MOP.
- 8% subsidy recovery.
- Whole-flat rental is never allowed.
- Resale buyers must meet the eligibility rules for buying a flat directly from HDB (GC 11.10), including the $16k family income ceiling.
- No Plus or Prime flat has reached MOP yet, so the resale discount is untested [Estimate].

**Estimate at MOP (~2040–41, in today's dollars)** [Estimate]
- **4-room, gross:** about **$820k–$945k**. Method: today's ~10-year-old comparables (median ~$992k), less a 5–15% Plus discount, less ~4% for the smaller 89 sqm floor area.
- **4-room, net after 8%:** about **$755k–$870k**, or roughly **$140k–$230k** above the purchase price, like for like.
- **Nominal 2040 value** with 0–2%/yr growth: about $820k–$1.25M gross. Resale prices were flat in 2026 [Unverified, 99.co Q3 flash −0.2%].
- **2-room Flexi Type 2:** gross about $400k–$500k, net about $370k–$460k, against $306k–$378k paid.
- **Short-lease 2-room Flexi:** cannot be resold [Official].
- **View:** modest, capped upside. The value is in the home, not the investment.

## 4. BTO type and stipulations

**Primary sources:** HDB General Conditions for Purchase (GC, Nov 2026), the MyNiceHome classification guide, and HDB Annex A on income ceilings (Aug 2026).

| Rule | What applies | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **MOP** | **10 years**, with physical and continuous occupation (GC 11.5a). Standard flats: 5 years | [Official] |
| **Subsidy recovery** | **8%** of the higher of resale price or valuation. Also charged on surrender, compulsory acquisition and the Lease Buyback Scheme (GC 11.6). Added on top of any resale levy and paid before CPF refunds and the loan. Joint tenants are equally liable; tenants-in-common pay by share. **Does not apply to short-lease 2-room Flexi** | [Official] |
| **Whole-flat rental** | **Never allowed**, even after MOP (GC 11.5 footnote; MyNiceHome) | [Official] |
| **Room rental** | Spare bedrooms can be rented during the MOP if you meet HDB's eligibility rules (MyNiceHome). Whether 2-room Flexi flats qualify is not confirmed | [Official] / [Unverified] for 2RF |
| **Private property** | Not allowed during the 10-year MOP, in Singapore or overseas (GC 11.5b). No rule found against it after MOP, but the flat still cannot be rented out | [Official] / [Unverified] after MOP |
| **Another new HDB flat or new EC** | Not allowed during the MOP (GC 11.5c) | [Official] |
| **Who can buy on resale** | Only buyers who meet the prevailing eligibility conditions for buying a flat directly from HDB (GC 11.10) | [Official] |
| **Resale buyers: income ceiling** | **Settled: $16,000.** Annex A raises the ceiling for "Resale HDB Plus/Prime flat without CPF Housing Grant" from $14k to **$16k** for families, and for singles buying a resale Plus flat without the Singles Grant from $14k to **$16k**. Applies to HFE applications from 24 Aug 2026. Extended families: $24k | [Official] |
| **Resale buyers: private property** | A 30-month wait-out after selling private property, by analogy with the rules for buying from HDB | [Estimate] |
| **Resale buyers: singles** | Singles aged 35+ may buy resale Plus flats (Annex A has a column for them) | [Official] |
| **Resale buyers: MOP** | Not confirmed whether they serve a fresh 10-year MOP | [Unverified] |
| **Clawback on later resales** | Reportedly paid once, by the original owner only | [Unverified] |
| **Family Care Scheme (Proximity)** | Obligations run 10 years for Plus flats | [Official] |
| **Singles 35+** | Can apply for 2-room Flexi ($8k ceiling; EHG up to $60k) | [Official] |
| **Short-lease 2-room Flexi (55+)** | No resale and no rental. Can only be returned to HDB for a refund of the remaining lease value (GC 2.2/2.3). Family ceiling $16k. Any private property must be sold within 6 months of key collection | [Official] |

## 6. Summary

**Pros**
- About a 3–6 minute walk to Mattar (Downtown Line), with the CBD about 25–30 min door to door [Verified / Estimate].
- Canossa Catholic Primary is within 1 km of every block (~200–290 m), and Maris Stella (Primary) holding site is within 1 km [Verified].
- Two hawker centres within about 0.7–0.9 km on foot, and New Econ supermarket about 630 m away [Verified].
- Aljunied Park next door, a common green with a court, and a preschool on site [Official].
- About $285k–$465k below nearby young resale 4-rooms, which sell for $920k–$1.1M [Official / Verified].
- An 8% clawback, lower than Bayshore's 9% and Toa Payoh Hillside's 11% [Official].
- Small estate (438 units) and a 39-month wait [Official].

**Cons**
- 10-year MOP, no whole-flat rental ever, and an 8% clawback [Official].
- Resale buyers are restricted (BTO-style eligibility, $16k ceiling), and Plus resale values are untested [Official / Estimate].
- Only 2-room Flexi and 4-room flats, and the 4-room is a compact 89 sqm [Official].
- Circle Line and East-West Line stations are a 17–20 min walk [Verified].
- Few malls within walking distance; the hospitals are about 4–5 km away [Verified / Estimate].
- Construction next door until about 2029, plus a future high-rise site across the road [Official / Verified].
- Industrial pockets nearby and Geylang's reputation [Estimate].

**Who it suits**
- First-timer families who work along the Downtown Line or in the CBD, plan to stay at least 10 years, and value Canossa or Maris Stella primary priority.
- Singles aged 35+ and seniors who want a 2-room Flexi close to town.

**Does not suit**
- Anyone planning to buy private property within about 10 years.
- Anyone who may need to rent out the whole flat.
- Buyers looking to make money on resale.

**Verdict:** a strong own-stay pick with capped investment upside. Apply if the 10-year horizon fits. Expect 4-room first-timer odds of roughly 1.5–4× [Estimate]. If Cedar Primary matters to you, check its 1 km status for Blk 138A on OneMap once postal codes are released.
