# Chencharu Acres: Standard Yishun BTO, a 6–9 minute walk from Khatib MRT, in two phases

*Prepared 4 Nov 2026, launch day. Applications run from 4 to 11 Nov 2026 [Official, Annex B].*

**How to read the tags:**
- **[Official]** comes from HDB: the Flat Portal project page and site plan, Annex A or B, or the press release.
- **[Verified]** means I checked it against data or a reliable independent source. That includes data.gov.sg resale records, OneMap coordinates, OSRM walking routes and multiple news reports.
- **[Estimate]** is my own calculation or judgement.
- **[Unverified]** comes from a secondary source that I could not confirm.

### Rental flats inside the precinct [Official]

HDB says the project has "1 block dedicated to rental flats". Of the 11 sale blocks, 3 have 28 rental flats each and one has 14.

The site-plan table shows which blocks:

| Block | Rental flats | Sale flats |
|---|---|---|
| 996B | 210 (whole block) | – |
| 992A | 28 | 112 |
| 992B | 28 | 112 |
| 996C | 28 | 140 |
| 996A | 14 | 112 |
| 992C, 995A–C, 997A–C | 0 | all |

Rental flats make up about 16% of the homes in the precinct [Estimate].

What this means for buyers:
- **It is not unusual.** HDB has mixed public rental flats into new sale precincts and blocks in recent years, and Chencharu's masterplan aims for a mixed community [Estimate].
- **It is mainly a matter of preference.** If you would rather not share a block or lift lobby with rental flats, choose from 992C, 995A–C or 997A–C [Estimate].
- **Resale.** I found no data showing a resale discount for mixed blocks. Some buyers may still mark them down, so treat any effect as uncertain [Estimate].
- **Daily use.** Rental flats are 2-room-size units in HDB's public rental scheme. Residents share the precinct's facilities (MSCPs, preschools, central green, hard court), which HDB says are open to the public [Official].

### On-site facilities [Official, project page and site plan]

- **2 MSCPs** (blocks 994 and 999), each with a **preschool on the ground floor** and a roof garden with fitness facilities.
- A central green with playgrounds and fitness stations, and a hard court.
- Smart features: pneumatic waste conveyance, smart lighting, and homes wired for smart devices.
- Eco features: separate recycling chutes, regenerative lifts, bicycle stands and provision for EV chargers.
- Facilities are open to the public.
- HDB notes that visual screens may be needed where blocks face military premises. It also says residential blocks may be finished before precinct facilities.

### Competition and ballot odds

- Chencharu Acres is the **only Yishun project** in this exercise [Official]. The exercise has 7,964 flats in 7 projects [Official, press release].
- HDB shortlists 100–200% of supply. At least 90% of 4-room and larger flats go to first-timer families [Official].
- **HDB's published application rates do not cover Yishun.** HDB's application-rate service only returns the Feb and Jun 2026 exercises, and neither had a Yishun project [Official].
  - The closest comparisons are other Standard projects in the north.
  - **Woodgrove Acres** (Woodlands, Jun 2026), first-timer rates: 4-room 4.1, 5-room 2.5, 3-room 2.3. 2-room Flexi singles: 19.3.
  - **Sembawang** (Feb and Jun 2026): 4-room 0.6, 5-room 0.4, 3-room 0.7–1.4 [Official].
- **Chencharu Grove (Oct 2025)**, secondary sources only:
  - 5-room first-timer rate 3.3–3.4; 3-room 2.1.
  - The often-quoted "4-room 1.8, 5-room 3.7" appears to combine Chencharu Grove with Yishun Glade [Unverified].
  - Chencharu Hills (Jun 2024) reportedly drew about 4,630 applicants for 1,277 flats [Unverified].
- Huttons' forecast of more than 30,000 applicants and an overall rate above 3.7 is unverified [Unverified].
- **My estimate:** first-timer rates of about 1.5–3 for 4-room and 2–4 for 5-room, given 644 five-room units and the MRT walk. 2-room Flexi for singles is likely to be heavily oversubscribed (10 or more) [Estimate].

### 3e. Appreciation view [Estimate]

**In favour:**
- A built-in discount of about 25–35% against nearby resale.
- No clawback and the widest pool of resale buyers.
- A 6–9 minute walk to the MRT.
- The integrated development (hawker centre, bus interchange, shops) should be running by MOP.

**Against:**
- About 10,000 Chencharu homes will reach MOP in waves through the 2030s.
- Yishun prices have been flat since 2025.
- Blocks facing the MRT viaduct and Ave 2 may sell at a small discount for noise.
- Mixed rental and sale blocks may put off some buyers.

**Likely premium units:** high floors with open views, and blocks away from the viaduct.

## 4. BTO type and stipulations: Standard

Chencharu Acres is a **Standard** project [Official]. The Standard / Plus / Prime framework applies to launches from Oct 2024.

| Rule | Standard (this project) | Plus / Prime, for contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum occupation period (MOP) | **5 years** [Official] | 10 years [Official] |
| Subsidy recovery on resale | **None** [Official] | Yes; Plus recovers less than Prime [Official] |
| Renting out the whole flat | **Allowed after MOP**, under HDB rental rules [Official] | Never [Official] |
| Renting out bedrooms | Allowed during MOP if you meet the eligibility rules and live in the flat [Official] | Same |
| During MOP (all HDB flats) | You cannot sell the flat, rent out the whole flat, buy private residential property, or apply for another BTO [Official] | Same |
| Who can buy on resale | General resale rules apply, including the Ethnic Integration Policy and SPR quotas. Resale buyers face no income ceiling; income limits apply only to grants and HDB loans [Unverified that nothing has changed]. | Tighter rules. **Not settled:** reports put the Plus/Prime resale buyer ceiling at $14k. HDB's housing-loan page reportedly says Prime resale eligibility "follows the prevailing BTO eligibility criteria", which would mean $16k for families and $8k for singles from 24 Aug 2026. I found no official page that states the figure, so I cannot settle it [Unverified]. **Neither figure applies to this Standard project.** |
| Short-lease 2-room Flexi | Cannot be sold on the open market or rented out whole [Unverified] | – |

**Application rules for this exercise [Official, Annex B]:**
- **Eligibility:** you need a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter. The old report's "applications made by 25 Sep 2026 were prioritised" is **not in Annex B**, and sources disagree on the date (25 Sep vs 15 Sep) [Unverified]. Check your own HFE letter.
- **One application per household:** one flat type in one town, with a $10 fee.
- **Ballot:** HDB shortlists 100–200% of supply. At least 90% of 4-room and larger flats are reserved for first-timer families.
- **If you are invited to book but don't:** first-timers are treated as second-timers for 1 year.
- **Ballot chances:** reset in Oct 2024. Only unsuccessful applications for Standard projects count towards extra ballot chances.
- **Queue positions:** released in Jan 2027. Booking starts in Mar 2027, tentatively.
- **Agreement for Lease:** signed within 9 months of booking.
- **Downpayment:**
  - 10% with an HDB loan
  - 20% with a bank loan
  - 2.5% for couples on deferred income assessment
- **Loan limit:** loan-to-value is capped at 75%.

## 6. Summary

### Pros

- **Standard flat:** 5-year MOP, no clawback, whole-flat rental allowed after MOP.
- **Good value:** a 4-room from $353k against nearby young resale of about $620k (median, 58 sales). The discount is about 35% on 4-room flats and 25–30% on 5-room flats.
- **Walkable MRT:** Khatib MRT is a **6–9.5 minute walk** depending on block, and the CBD is about 30 minutes by train with no transfer. There are buses on Ave 2 and a future bus interchange across Yishun Walk.
- **Primary school:** Peiying Primary is within 1 km of every block. Naval Base Primary is borderline.
- **Healthcare:** Khatib Polyclinic is a 10-minute walk, and two hospitals are about 1.5 km away.
- **Leisure:** the park connector runs along the site's edge, Lower Seletar Reservoir is about a 12-minute walk, and the Yishun Sport Centre and HomeTeamNS Khatib are across the road.
- **Supply:** 644 five-room flats, and 2 preschools on site.

### Cons

- **Long wait:** 39–41 months, with keys in 3Q–4Q 2030. The contractual DPD is 2032.
- **Finishes are extra:** about $3k–9.6k depending on flat type.
- **Rental flats:** one block (996B) is entirely rental, and four sale blocks include rental flats (308 in total).
- **Noise:** the elevated MRT line and Yishun Ave 2 run along the east side, next to the 992 blocks.
- **No hawker centre on foot today:** the nearest is about 2.6 km away. The planned one has no date.
- **Construction:** around the estate for most of the 2030s.
- **Resale outlook:** large Chencharu supply reaching MOP, and Yishun prices flat since 2025.
- **3-room buyers:** all 3-room flats are in the later, 41-month group.

### Who it suits

- First-timer families who want an affordable 4-room or 5-room within a walk of the MRT and a primary school, and who can wait until about 2030.
- People who work along the North-South Line or in the north.
- Singles (2-room Flexi) and seniors (short-lease 2-room Flexi) who want to be near a polyclinic and hospitals.

It is less suitable if:
- you need a home before 2031;
- you are sensitive to train noise and could not avoid the eastern blocks;
- you mind mixed rental and sale blocks and might not get a ballot number good enough to choose;
- you want a mature-estate setting with a hawker centre nearby straight away.

### Verdict [Estimate]

A solid, good-value Standard BTO.
- **Its strengths:** a short walk to Khatib MRT (shorter than the earlier Chencharu projects), Peiying Primary within 1 km, and a resale discount of about 35% for 4-room flats.
- **Its trade-offs:** a wait of about 3.5–4 years, no finishes included, some train noise, rental flats mixed into the precinct, and amenities that arrive later.

**Block choice matters:**
- **996/997 blocks:** earlier completion, further from the viaduct, slightly longer walk.
- **992/995 blocks:** later completion, nearest the MRT, but noisier, and the only blocks with 3-room flats.
