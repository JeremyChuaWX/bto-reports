**Likely classification [Estimate]**
- **Standard, about 55–60%.** The site sits inside a 1982–85 neighbourhood, 10 min from Simei MRT and 14 min from Tampines EW2. Nearby Tampines Bliss (St 22, Feb 2026) and Simei Symphony (Jul 2025) were both Standard **[Official]**.

**3.5 CCAs have no resale value**
- CCAs cannot be sold or rented out, in whole or in part **[Official, HDB conditions of purchase]**.
- When you return the flat, HDB refunds the value of the remaining lease (see 4.2).
- Treat a CCA as prepaid housing plus care, not as an investment.

## 4. BTO type and stipulations

**4.1 Standard / Plus / Prime and 4-room terms [Official, MND classification page (updated 17 Sep 2026) and Nov 2026 Annex B]**

| | Standard | Plus | Prime |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOP | 5 years | 10 years | 10 years |
| Resale buyers | All eligible buyers, including PRs and singles | At least one SC; income ≤ $16k (families and singles); no private property in the past 30 months | At least one SC; income ≤ $16k (families) or $8k (singles); no private property in the past 30 months |
| Clawback | None | Fixed % set at launch, on first resale only | Same |
| Resale income ceiling history | – | Raised from $14k on 24 Aug 2026 | Same |
| Renting | Rooms during MOP while living there; whole flat after MOP | Rooms only, never the whole flat | Same |
| Example 4-room EHG | $80k | $55k | $55k |

**4-room application terms [Official, Nov 2026 Annex B]:**
- Household income ceiling $16,000, or $24,000 for extended families.
- At least 90% of 4-room and larger flats are reserved for first-timer families, with up to 10% for second-timers.
- $2,000 option fee; HDB loan up to 75% of the price.
- First-timers who don't book a flat when invited are put in the second-timer category for one year.
- Extra ballot chances build up only from unsuccessful tries in Standard projects; counts were reset in Oct 2024.
- **New from Feb 2027:** first-timer families get one extra ballot chance for each Singapore Citizen child aged 18 or under **[Official, HDB Aug 2026]**.

**Applying with a parent:** I could not confirm a Multi-Generation Priority Scheme route to a CCA. HDB's Family Care Scheme (Joint Balloting) covers only 2-room Flexi to 5-room flats; CCA joint balloting is limited to up to two CCA households, and CCA applicants who need help with daily activities get priority **[Official, GCP]**. Plan for a parent (CCA) and child (4-room) to apply **separately** unless HDB says otherwise at launch. Only about 9 of the 90 4-room flats can go to second-timers.

**4.2 Community Care Apartments**

**What it is [Official]**
- A senior-friendly studio (39 sqm, 36 sqm internal at Toa Payoh Hillside) with a compulsory Basic Service Package (BSP) from an appointed operator.
- HDB calls Toa Payoh Hillside "the sixth CCA project", so this would be the seventh **[Estimate]**. The earlier ones are Harmony Village @ Bukit Batok, Queensway Canopy, Chai Chee Green, Merpati Alcove and Fernvale Plains.

**Eligibility [Official, MOH/HDB Jul 2026, Nov 2026 Annex B and GCP]**
- **Minimum age 55** (lowered from 65), applying from the Nov 2026 exercise. MOH's 13 Jul release said "October 2026" because the exercise was later moved to November (HDB, 23 Aug 2026).
- Household income ceiling **$16,000**.
- Open to first- and second-timers. Private property owners can apply but must sell it within 6 months of key collection.
- No HDB loan: pay in cash and/or CPF. Using CPF beyond the usual limits requires setting aside the Basic Retirement Sum and CPF Board approval.
- **$500 option fee.** Annex B says you sign the BSP agreement with the operator at booking; the conditions of purchase refer to key collection. Check at launch.

**Lease options [Official, GCP]**
- 15 to 45 years, in 5-year steps.
- The lease must last until the youngest owner is at least 95.

| Youngest owner's age | Shortest lease allowed |
|---|---|
| 55–59 | 40 yr |
| 60–64 | 35 yr |
| 65–69 | 30 yr |
| 70–74 | 25 yr |
| 75–79 | 20 yr |
| 80+ | 15 yr |

A 55-year-old can therefore only choose 40 or 45 years, which makes the flat noticeably more expensive than the 30-year leases older buyers used to take.

**Basic Service Package [Official, MOH Jul 2026; operator page]**
- **Compulsory.** It is charged monthly on top of the flat price; HDB's price lists exclude it.
- **Covers:** simple home repairs, help with letters and bills, a 24-hour emergency response (alert device now optional), and help arranging paid add-ons such as caregiving, day care and housekeeping. Social activities have moved to Active Ageing Centres.
- **Fees for existing CCAs** are being cut by 18–75% from Q2 2027, e.g. Bukit Batok from $159 to $130–$140, Queensway from $175–$185 to $107–$117, Chai Chee from $175–$185 to $104–$114, and Merpati/Fernvale from $185–$195 to $111–$121 a month.
- **Toa Payoh Hillside**, the first project priced under the new model, charges **$61–$71 a month before GST and subsidy** **[Official, operator page via go.gov.sg/basicservicepackage]**. This project is likely to be similar, at about $60–$75 **[Estimate]**. Over a 40-year lease, $61–$71 a month comes to about $29k–$34k before GST and subsidy.
- **Subsidies** are means-tested and only for residents who cannot do at least one daily-living activity (e.g. bathing) unaided:

  | Monthly income per household member | SC born 1969 or earlier | SC born after 1969 | PR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | up to $1,500 | 95% | 80% | 55% |
  | $1,501–$2,300 | 85% | 70% | 45% |
  | $2,301–$2,600 | 75% | 60% | 35% |
  | $2,601–$3,600 | 55% | 40% | 20% |
  | $3,601–$4,800 | 35% | 20% | 10% |
  | over $4,800 | 0% | 0% | 0% |

  Households with no income get the top rate if the home's annual value is $21,000 or less, and nothing otherwise.

**Grant and payment [Official]**
- EHG of up to $120k. HDB's examples use $120k.
- At least 5% of the price must be paid in cash or CPF.
- For a buyer eligible for the full grant, a 40-yr CCA here might cost about $5k–$45k net **[Estimate]**.

**No resale, no rental [Official, GCP]:** the flat cannot be sold on the open market or rented out, whether a room or the whole flat.

**Returning the flat:** HDB "will refund the value of the remaining lease" **[Official, GCP]**. Third-party sources (teeco.sg) say this is straight-line, with the lower of pro-rated value or 95% if returned within 12 months; e.g. a $108k 35-yr lease returned after 10 years would refund about $77k **[Unverified]**. Refunds go to CPF and/or cash under HDB's usual rules.

**On death [Unverified]:** a surviving co-owner would normally stay on. What happens after the last owner dies (presumably a remaining-lease refund to the estate) wasn't found in any official source; confirm with HDB.

## 6. Summary

**Pros**
- **Hospitals close by:** CGH 15 min, St Andrew's Community Hospital 16 min, CGH Medical Centre 18 min and Tampines Polyclinic about 20 min, with a proposed health/medical site right next door.
- **Mature-estate convenience:** CC next door (2–3 min), Round Market 7 min, FairPrice about 6 min (unconfirmed), Eastpoint 12 min, Tampines Mall 14 min.
- **Two East-West Line stations:** Simei 10 min via a PIE underpass, and Tampines EW2 14 min. Four Downtown Line stations are 19–27 min away.
- **Schools:** four primary schools within 1 km straight line, ten within 2 km.
- The Tampines Park Connector is right outside.
- It is a small, single-block project.
- **CCA rules have improved in 2026:** minimum age 55, much cheaper care package (Toa Payoh Hillside $61–$71 a month), and a grant of up to $120k.

**Cons**
- No shops or eating house in the project.
- Not at an MRT station. The hospitals are close in a straight line, but the walk is about twice as long because of the PIE.
- Possible noise from the MRT viaduct (east) and the PIE (south-east).
- **Only 90 4-room flats.** Tampines 4-room drew about 10.3 applicants per flat in Feb 2026, and 39 per flat among second-timers.
- **If Plus:** 10-year MOP, no whole-flat rental ever, a 6–9% clawback and fewer resale buyers.
- **CCA:** no resale or rental, and a 55-year-old must take a 40- or 45-year lease. The care-package fee runs for the whole lease. How the refund is calculated is officially only "value of the remaining lease".
- Parent-and-child joint balloting across a CCA and a 4-room is not confirmed.

**Who it suits**
- Seniors aged 55+ in the east who want a care-supported studio near CGH and value that over capital gains.
- Families who want a parent nearby: they may get the same block, but should expect to apply separately.
- Young first-timer families wanting a mature estate with many schools and an East-West Line commute.
- Less suited to buyers chasing resale gains or rental income, second-timers (very low odds), or anyone wanting an MRT at the doorstep.

**Verdict [Opinion]**
- **For CCA buyers,** this is one of the best-located CCA sites so far: hospital, polyclinic, market and CC are all within about 20 minutes on foot. The lower cost of the new care package makes long leases much more affordable.
- **For 4-room buyers,** it's a solid mature-estate choice, and the value depends on the classification:
  - **If Standard,** an estimated $490k–$630k against a likely $740k–$840k MOP value (today's dollars) is attractive.
  - **If Plus,** it is still reasonable, but you're locked in for 10 years and pay a clawback on resale.
- Look again when HDB publishes prices and the classification in Feb 2027.

**Still unconfirmed:** project name, classification, prices, waiting time and completion; care-package fee and operator; block position; whether the 330 includes rental flats; the health/medical site; zoning; CCA + 4-room joint balloting; refund details and death rules; nearest bus stops and Active Ageing Centre; train times.
