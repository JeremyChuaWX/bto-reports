## 2. Amenities

**How distances were measured:**
- Straight-line distances are from the site centre, using OneMap.
- Walking distances are OSRM foot routes on today's network [Estimate]. The site is a closed former police compound with no public paths, so OSRM starts the route on Mount Pleasant Rd, about 210 m north of the site centre.
- Many routes therefore detour via Mount Pleasant Rd and Thomson Rd, and come out about 1.8-2.4 times the straight line. They should shorten once the estate's roads and the green spine open.
- School distances are approximate. MOE measures home-to-school-boundary.

- Mount Pleasant Crest, in the same estate, was Prime at 12% [Official].

- The old $14k figure in secondary sources is out of date.

**Other points:**
- From the Feb 2027 exercise, first-timer families get an extra ballot chance for each citizen child aged 18 or under (2027-02 README) [Unverified].
- The loan-to-value limit is 75% [Unverified].

## 5. Construction stage and timeline

**What is on the plot now:**
- I could not get current site imagery.
- HDB lists the project as an upcoming launch (stage "L") [Official].
- I found no award of a main building contract for "C5" [Unverified].
- A GeBIZ-derived listing shows "Specialist Accredited Checking Services for Mount Pleasant C5-C6" (HDB000ETT26000019) awarded on 2 Mar 2026 to CPG Consultants for S$60,000 [Unverified].
- The equivalent C1-C4 checking contract was awarded on 12 Feb 2026 [Unverified]. This suggests design work and approvals for C5 are under way, but foundation work has probably not started.
- The site sits on the former OPA grounds next to conserved blocks. Expect some site clearance and possible heritage-related constraints.

**Neighbouring works:**
- Mount Pleasant Crest (C1-C4) is next door.
- A search snippet mentions "HDB Building Works at Mount Pleasant Contracts 1 to 4 comprising 1,625 DUs ($386.80m)" [Unverified].

**Timeline [Estimate]:**

| Milestone | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Launch | Feb 2027 [Verified] |
| Estimated waiting time | about 50-58 months. Mount Pleasant Crest was 59 months from Oct 2025, and this contract is roughly a month behind it in design. Hillside was 45 months. |
| Key collection | about mid-2031 to late 2031 |
| TE10 opening | with the first housing, probably about 2030 (no official date) |
| Neighbourhood centre / bus interchange | progressive, after the first completions |

**Risks:**
1. A long wait of about 4.5 years or more. Heritage work and an estate-wide build could add delay.
2. Early residents may face years of construction from later Mount Pleasant phases (about 6,000 flats in total).
3. Amenities will be thin at first: no hawker centre within 1.4 km, and long detours on foot until the internal roads open.
4. TE10's opening date is not guaranteed.
5. Integrated rental blocks: sold and rental flats share the blocks.

## 6. Summary

**Pros**
- Mount Pleasant MRT (TEL) at the doorstep: about 8 min to Orchard and about 20 min to Shenton Way / Marina Bay [Estimate].
- A rare city-fringe estate on heritage grounds, with conserved 1920s-30s buildings, a parade-square plaza, a green spine and a car-lite design.
- Next to Novena's medical cluster: Thomson Medical 0.8 km, TTSH 1.5 km, and a health plot planned next door.
- Popular schools nearby: CHIJ Primary 0.8 km; ACS (Primary), SCGS and Marymount about 1.2-1.3 km.
- MacRitchie and Bukit Brown about 1.4 km.
- 1,140 units, including 600 4-room, so odds are better than in small projects.
- Strong nearby resale benchmarks: 2018-lease 4-room flats at a median of $1.13M, against a likely BTO price of about $570k-$820k.

**Cons**
- Likely Prime: 10-year MOP, about 12% subsidy recovery, no whole-flat rental, and a restricted resale buyer pool.
- A long wait of about 50-58 months [Estimate].
- No hawker centre or mall within walking distance at first; working MRT stations are more than 1 km away until TE10 opens.
- Living in a construction zone for years as later phases are built.
- Prices are likely at the upper end of Toa Payoh BTO prices.

**Suits:**
- Owner-occupiers planning to stay 10 years or more.
- CBD, Orchard or TEL commuters.
- Families who value CHIJ, ACS, SCGS or Marymount.
- Healthcare workers at Novena.
- Elderly buyers wanting a 2-room Flexi near hospitals.

**Does not suit:**
- People who want to rent out the whole flat or upgrade within 10 years.
- PR-only households.
- Anyone who needs keys before about 2031.
- Anyone who needs hawker food and a mall on their doorstep from day one.

**Verdict (opinion):** one of the strongest Feb 2027 sites for long-term owner-occupiers, if you can accept Prime terms and a wait of about 4.5 years. Confirm the classification, recovery rate, prices and waiting time at launch.
