# Tengah Brickland BTO (Feb 2027): next to the future Brickland NS3A station

*Research date: 4 Nov 2026. Project name not yet announced. HDB code TG_D5C3.*

**Labels used in this report:**
- **[Official]**: from HDB, LTA, MOE, MOH or MOT, including the project XML and the Nov 2026 town map.
- **[Verified]**: confirmed by two or more independent good sources, or computed by me from official data.
- **[Estimate]**: my own projection.
- **[Unverified]**: a single secondary source, or not yet published.

This is a separate project from **Plantation Dew**, the Tengah project in the Nov 2026 exercise.

## 1. Overview

### Exercise
- **Feb 2027 BTO exercise [Official]:** HDB's Flat Portal says the next BTO exercise, of about 4,300 flats, is in February 2027. A Sale of Balance Flats exercise of about 5,000 flats runs alongside it.
- **HFE deadline [Official]:** submit your HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter application by **15 Dec 2026** to be able to apply.
- **This site:** HDB lists it as an upcoming BTO, and the town map was issued in Nov 2026 [Official]. Media report the towns as Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh [Verified].
- **Exact launch date:** not yet announced [Unverified].

### Site [Official: project XML and town map]
- **Location:** lat 1.36983, lng 103.74633, in Tengah's **Brickland district** on the north-east edge of the town, next to Bukit Batok West.
- **Roads:**
  - Brickland Crescent (under construction) runs along the west and north-west.
  - Brickland Close (under construction) runs along the north-east.
  - Bukit Batok Road is to the south-east, with a strip for a proposed place of worship in between.
- **Next to the site:**
  - a proposed Health & Medical Care plot at the south-west corner
  - a proposed park and a proposed community garden
  - a utility plot (under construction)
  - a second Health & Medical Care plot across Brickland Road, near Choa Chu Kang Grove
- **Across Bukit Batok Road (all interim uses):** Home Team NS Adventure Centre, Bukit Batok Driving Centre and Bukit Batok Golf Range.
- **Brickland Weave** (Feb 2023 BTO) is about 170 m north-west [Verified].

### Likely classification: Standard [Estimate]
- **Precedent:** Plantation Dew (Nov 2026) is "Standard", with clawback "Not applicable" [Official].
- **Site profile:** a non-mature western town, 32% 2-room Flexi, and blocks mixing sold and rental flats.
- **Why Plus is not ruled out:** the MRT station is within walking distance, though it opens only in 2034. The Nov 2026 exercise had 3 Standard, 3 Plus and 1 Prime project.
- **When it will be known:** HDB announces the classification at launch [Official].

## 4. BTO type and stipulations

The classification is announced at launch. Standard is likely [Estimate]. The rules below are from HDB's pages, updated Aug–Nov 2026 [Official].

| Condition | Standard (likely) | Plus | Prime |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOP | **5 years** | 10 years | 10 years |
| Resale buyers: citizenship | Open market: an SC or SPR plus at least one SC/SPR occupant | Same as BTO: an SC plus at least one other SC or SPR | Same as BTO (follows prevailing BTO eligibility) |
| Resale buyers: income ceiling | **None**, unless taking CPF grants or an HDB loan | **Same as new flats:** $16,000 for 4-/5-room ($24,000 for extended families); $8,000 for 2-room Flexi; singles $16,000 | Prevailing BTO ceilings; singles limited to 2-room ($8,000) |
| Resale buyers: private property | Must sell any private property within 6 months of buying | Must not own any; 30-month wait after selling | Same as Plus |
| Subsidy recovery on sale | **None** | % of the higher of resale price or valuation, set at launch (8–11% in Nov 2026 projects, AsiaOne) | Yes, higher than Plus |
| Renting out the whole flat | **Allowed after MOP**, with HDB approval | Never | Never |
| Renting out rooms | 3-room and larger: spare bedrooms with HDB approval, even during MOP. No provision for 2-room Flexi. | Same | Same |
| Buying private property | Only after MOP (SC households) | Only after the 10-year MOP | Only after the 10-year MOP |
| Singles aged 35+ | 2-room Flexi | 2-room Flexi | 2-room Flexi |

**The "$14k ceiling" question is settled.** HDB's eligibility page shows new flats and resale Plus flats in a single column, with the $16,000 / $8,000 ceilings that apply from 24 Aug 2026 [Official]. The $14k figure is the old pre-Aug 2026 ceiling.

**Other conditions:**
- **HFE eligibility [Official]:** income ceiling of $16,000 for families ($24,000 for extended families) and $8,000 for 2-room Flexi or singles. Must not have sold private property in the 30 months before applying.
- **Occupation:** you must live in the flat throughout the MOP [Official].
- **Resale quotas:** the Ethnic Integration Policy and the PR quota apply at resale [Official].
- **HDB loan:** up to 75% of the price [Unverified here; confirm in your HFE letter].
- **Ballot:** from Feb 2027, first-timer families get one extra ballot chance per SC child aged 18 or below [Verified, NDR 2026 coverage]. HDB's own text is not yet published.
- **Past application rates:** HDB shows no Tengah rates in the last year [Official]. The Feb 2023 Tengah projects drew 2.84x overall [Unverified, EdgeProp].
- **Centralised cooling:** used at other Tengah precincts, including Plantation Dew. Not confirmed for this site [Unverified].

## 6. Summary

**Pros**
- About a 5-minute walk to NS3A (from 2034), on the North-South Line straight to Jurong East and the city.
- On the established Bukit Batok West and Keat Hong edge: Keat Hong Shopping Centre is 0.9 km away and St Anthony's Primary is within 1 km.
- Likely Standard: 5-year MOP and no subsidy recovery.
- 440 5-room flats, a large share.
- The ACS(P) Tengah site (2030) is 1.24 km away; the Tengah hospital is due in the early 2030s.
- Young flats nearby resell for $660k–$700k (4-room).

**Cons**
- NS3A opens about 3–4 years after keys. Until then, the nearest MRT is Bukit Gombak, a 19-minute walk.
- Station construction next door, plus noise from Bukit Batok Road.
- No on-site shops announced; groceries are 1 km or more away.
- Tengah's own amenities, the town centre and the JRL are behind schedule.
- 32% 2-room Flexi, and some blocks mix sold and rental flats.
- Likely priced at the top of recent Tengah launches.
- Many nearby flats reach MOP in the 2030s.

**Who it suits**
- First-timer families wanting a 4- or 5-room flat in the west.
- People working in Jurong East, the Jurong Lake District, the Jurong Innovation District, Woodlands or along the North-South Line.
- Buyers who value Standard-flat flexibility.
- **Less suited to** anyone who needs an MRT station within walking distance from the day they move in.

**Verdict:** an above-average Tengah site. From 2034 its rail access will be among the best in Tengah. The trade-offs are the gap before the station opens, construction next door, and likely high-end pricing for Tengah.
