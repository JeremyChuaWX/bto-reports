Prepared 4 Nov 2026. HDB has published the unit mix, site and facilities. It has **not yet published** the project name, prices, classification or waiting time. Tags used:
- **[Official]**: from an HDB, URA, LTA, MOE/NEA (data.gov.sg) or other government source.
- **[Verified]**: checked independently, using data.gov.sg resale data, OneMap coordinates, OSRM walking routes, the URA Master Plan 2019 land-use data, or several consistent news reports.
- **[Estimate]**: my own projection or opinion.
- **[Unverified]**: from a secondary source or general knowledge, not confirmed.

### 1.2 Likely classification: Standard [Estimate]
- Simei Symphony next door, at the same station, is Standard [Official, Jul 2025 annex].
- The project offers 5-room flats. HDB's Nov 2026 5-room grant example is given only for Standard projects [Official].
- The site is outside the town centre. By comparison, Tampines Nova next to Tampines MRT is Plus (6% clawback), while Tampines Bliss is Standard [Official].
- Plus is still possible: the DTL is 4–6 min away, and Changi Business Park, Expo and the future T5 are nearby. Prime is very unlikely.

- **If it is Plus:** prices are likely similar or slightly lower. Tampines Nova (Plus) priced 4-room at $459k–$602k [Official]. In return you accept a 10-year MOP and a 6–9% clawback when you sell.
- **Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG):** HDB's Nov 2026 price illustrations assumed these amounts [Official]:
  - 2-room Flexi: $120k
  - 3-room: $105k (Standard) or $90k (Plus/Prime)
  - 4-room: $80k (Standard) or $55k (Plus/Prime)
  - 5-room: $55k (Standard)
  
  These are illustrative only. The actual grant depends on household income; the same release gives about $80k at $4,000 a month, $30k at $7,000 and $5k at $9,000 [Official].
- **Income ceilings:** $16,000 for families and $8,000 for singles, for HFE (HDB Flat Eligibility) letter applications made from 24 Aug 2026 [Official].

### 1.4 Exercise logistics
- You need a valid HFE letter. Submit it and all documents by 15 Dec 2026 [Official].
- A Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise of about 5,000 flats runs at the same time [Official].
- **From the Feb 2027 exercise**, first-timer families with or expecting children get one extra ballot chance per Singapore Citizen child aged 18 or below, for all BTO and SBF applications [Official, HDB National Day Rally 2026 measures].
- **Expected competition:** In Feb 2026, Tampines 4-room drew 7.2 first-timer applicants per flat (Bliss and Nova combined, 3,484 applicants for 337 flats). Tampines Nova 2-room Flexi drew 4.0 for first-timer families and 117.5 for singles [Official, HDB past application rates]. With only 120 each of 4-room and 5-room here, expect heavy oversubscription [Estimate].

### 3.5 Outlook for price growth [Estimate]
**Positives:**
- 4–6 min to the DTL; one change to the TEL from 26 Dec 2026; TEL at Expo from the mid-2030s.
- Jobs nearby: Changi Business Park, the airport and T5, Expo, SUTD, CGH.
- A new lease next to an ageing Simei estate, whose flats sell for $608k–$640k for a 4-room.

**Negatives:**
- No hawker centre within about 1.8 km.

- Simei Symphony's 380 flats reach MOP around 2034. The newly upzoned plots south of Upper Changi Link and at Simei Lane may add more new supply.

**Net view:** growth broadly in line with, or slightly above, newer Standard flats in Tampines.

## 6. Summary

**Pros**
- 4–6 min walk to Upper Changi (DTL) and about 11 min to Expo; 3 stops to Tampines Hub; DTL–TEL interchange at Sungei Bedok from 26 Dec 2026.
- Eating house, minimart, shops, preschool and a park under construction right at the site [Official].
- Close to CGH, Changi Business Park, SUTD, Expo and two malls within about 1.25 km on foot.
- Likely Standard: 5-year MOP, whole-flat rental after MOP, no clawback.
- Has 5-room flats; clear price reference from Simei Symphony next door.

**Cons**
- No hawker centre within about 1.8 km (2.3 km on foot).
- About a 13-minute walk to the EWL; about 2.2 km from the town centre.
- Only Changkat Primary is within 1 km in a straight line.
- Polyclinic is about 3 km away.
- Small 4- and 5-room supply (120 each) against strong demand: Tampines 4-room drew 7.2 first-timers per flat in Feb 2026.
- Risk of Plus classification.
- The area will densify after the upzonings, with more construction nearby.

**Who it suits:**
- Families working around Changi Business Park, the airport and T5, Expo, CGH, SUTD, or along the DTL.
- Buyers who want a 5-room flat in a mature eastern estate.

**Less suited to:** people who eat at hawker centres daily, or who need a direct EWL ride or a primary school within 1 km.

**Verdict [Estimate]**
- **If Standard:** a strong pick, especially 4- and 5-room, if you can wait 3.5–4 years. Families with young children benefit from the new extra ballot chance.
- **If Plus:** much less attractive. The location premium is modest compared with the cost of a 10-year MOP and the clawback.
- **At launch, check:** the classification, the waiting time, which blocks face the roads, and which primary schools fall within 1 km of each block.
