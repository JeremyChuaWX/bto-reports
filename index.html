  • Resale income ceiling for Plus and Prime buyers:
    • $16,000 for families and $24,000 for extended families;
    • for singles, $16,000 for a Plus resale flat and $8,000 for a 2-room Prime resale flat.
    • This applies to HFE letters issued from 24 Aug 2026 (HDB Annex A, Aug 2026). Sources that still say $14k are out of date.
  • Clawback: paid on the first resale only. It is a percentage of the resale price or the valuation, whichever is higher.
  • Community Care Apartments:
    • You must be 55 or older.
    • Leases run 15–45 years, and must last until all owners reach age 95.
    • The Basic Service Package is compulsory. For Toa Payoh Hillside it is about $61–71 a month before GST and subsidy.
  • Key dates:
    • Nov 2026 applications close on 11 Nov 2026, 11:59 pm.
    • The deadline to get an HFE letter in time for Feb 2027 is 15 Dec 2026.
    • The Feb 2027 exercise has about 4,300 flats. From Feb 2027, first-timer families get an extra ballot chance for each child.
  • Transport milestones:
    • Bedok South, Sungei Bedok and Xilin MRT stations open on 26 Dec 2026.
    • JRL Stage 1 opens mid-2028.
    • The North–South Corridor slips to 2029.

Main corrections from the first-pass reports

  • Toa Payoh Hillside:
    • Keys are due 1Q 2031, with delivery by 30 Sep 2032, not 2030.
    • Caldecott MRT is 214–253 m away (3 min), not 120 m.
    • There are 232 rental flats.
    • 4-room flats are 90 m², or 93 m² for the balcony type.
  • Toa Payoh (Feb 2027): the site is the former Police Academy at Mount Pleasant, with 1,140 units. TE10 has no opening date, and there is no hawker centre within 1.4 km.
  • Tampines CCA:
    • Simei MRT is a 761 m walk via the PIE underpass.
    • The lease must last until age 95, so a 55-year-old can only take 40 or 45 years.
    • The care-package estimate fell from $100–140 a month to about $61–71.
    • Joint balloting for a CCA and a 4-room flat is unconfirmed.
  • Bayshore Sails: the “~5× oversubscribed” claim is not supported. The Oct 2024 Bayshore 4-room first-timer rate was about 2.1–2.5.
  • Bishan (Feb 2027): the first-pass report analysed the wrong site. The real site is Sin Ming Street / Marymount Rd, with 1,070 units (520 2-room Flexi, 80 3-room, 470 4-room). It is probably the shorter-waiting-time project, with keys around mid-2030. However, neither OneMap satellite imagery (undated) nor HDB’s under-construction dataset (15 Oct 2026) shows works on the site yet, so treat the timing as unconfirmed. It sits on the edge of an industrial estate, and HDB notes industries within 500 m. It is likely Plus rather than Prime. Blk 22 Sin Ming Rd is a coffee shop, not a hawker centre. Catholic High is within 1 km and Ai Tong is borderline.
  • Plus/Prime resale ceiling: settled at $16k (see above).

Verdicts in brief Estimate / opinion

  • Best value (Standard):
    • Sembawang Anchor: the fastest keys (1Q 2030) and the easiest first-timer odds, but a weak MRT link.
    • Chencharu Acres: a walkable MRT and about a 35% discount to resale.
    • Plantation Dew: good schools and a JRL station by 2028. It has the only 3Gen flats this round.
  • Best location (10-year commitment):
    • Toa Payoh Hillside: the best-connected project this round, but Prime terms make it an own-stay choice, not an investment.
    • Merpati Meadow and the Bayshore pair: Plus flats in strong locations, at a large discount to resale nearby.
  • Worth waiting for (Feb 2027):
    • Tampines (Upper Changi): if it launches as Standard.
    • Mount Pleasant: if you accept Prime terms and a wait of about 4.5 years.
    • Bishan (Sin Ming St): for fast keys in a mature central town, if you can live beside an industrial estate.
    • Tengah Brickland: for its rail access after 2034.

About the data

  • Project facts (classification, clawback, prices, waiting time, completion dates, unit supply, site coordinates) come from HDB's own project data on the HDB Flat Portal, retrieved 4 Nov 2026.
  • Maps use OneMap street and satellite tiles from the Singapore Land Authority. Rail lines come from URA Master Plan 2025 (amended Sep 2026); blocks under construction come from HDB's 'Under Construction Buildings' dataset.
  • Amenities come from agency datasets on data.gov.sg: LTA (MRT exits, bus stops), MOE (schools), NEA (hawker centres, supermarket licences), MOH (CHAS clinics, eldercare), ECDA (preschools), NParks (parks), PA (community clubs), NLB (libraries) and SportSG. Polyclinics and hospitals, and addresses without coordinates, are located with OneMap search.
  • Resale benchmarks are computed from HDB resale transactions on data.gov.sg (last 12 months).
  • Analysis (planned developments, malls, walking-route estimates, Feb 2027 estimates and verdicts) was researched by AI agents and then re-checked against official sources. Every fact in the reports is tagged Official, Verified, Estimate or Unverified.
