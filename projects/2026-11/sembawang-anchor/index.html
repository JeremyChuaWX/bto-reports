HDB's completion and delivery note

Estimated Completion Date (ECD) is the estimated date that the project will be completed. As ECD will depend on the progress of construction works on site, HDB shall not be responsible for any damage or loss arising from a change in the ECD. The ECD of this project is 1Q 2030. Delivery Possession Date (DPD) is the contractual date in the Agreement for Lease by which HDB is required to deliver vacant possession of the booked flat to buyers. DPD is later than the ECD. The estimated DPD for this project is 30 Sep 2031. HDB precincts are planned to serve the evolving needs of the community. There are spaces catered for future inclusion of amenities/ facilities (such as preschools, eldercare facilities, education centres, residents' network centres, day activity centres, etc), commercial facilities (such as shops and eating houses), mechanical and electrical rooms, and such other facilities depending on the prevailing needs, even after residents have moved into the precincts. These spaces could be in the void decks, common property, car parks, or stand-alone community buildings. In addition, housing projects may be located near roads, railways, airbases, construction sites, and land used for non-residential purposes including but not limited to industrial, commercial or utility uses. Site and layout

  • The broadest flat mix of this exercise’s three SWT projects: 2-room Flexi to 5-room, no 3Gen. 417 five-room flats (32%) is a high share Official.
  • Blk 432A combines sold flats and the 50 rental flats. It is also the block farthest from the MRT (1.24 km straight) Official / Verified.
  • Surroundings on HDB’s site plan Official:
    • North and west: public housing under construction.
    • North-east, across Admiralty Lane: a park under construction.
    • A site reserved for a future high-rise commercial and residential development (the Neighbourhood Centre).
    • East: a site reserved for a high-rise nursing home (up to about 19 storeys).
    • South-east: Rainbow Centre Admiral Hill School (special education).
    • South: existing blocks 484/485/492/493 Admiralty Link.
    • Across Sembawang Drive: Northoaks Primary and MK@Northoaks.
    • Future bus shelters on Sembawang Drive and possible bus bays on Admiralty Lane.
  • Check the brochure for west-sun stacks and stacks facing the nursing-home site.

3d. Estimated resale at MOP Estimate

Timing:

  • Keys ~1Q 2030, so the earliest resale is ~1Q 2035, with ~94 years of lease left.
  • If handover slips toward the 30 Sep 2031 delivery date, MOP moves to ~2036.

Method:

  • Today’s young-lease comparables (Montreal Link, the 2018–22 cohort, HDB’s Annex A figures), grown at 0–1.5% a year.
  • The low case knocks 5% off for the supply wave.
Type Mid BTO price Comparable today Est. resale ~2035 Indicative paper gain
3-room ~$311k $500k – $528k $475k – $600k ~$165k – $290k
4-room ~$370k $589k – $665k $560k – $750k ~$190k – $380k
5-room ~$547k $760k – $823k $720k – $905k ~$175k – $360k

What these gains leave out:

  • Agent fees, renovation, and interest on loans.
  • CPF used plus accrued interest goes back into your CPF account. The EHG does not have to be repaid.

What supports prices:

  • Young-lease Sembawang 4-rooms already trade at $610k–$665k.
  • The Shipyard waterfront plan and growth in Woodlands.

What holds them back:

  • Supply wave: most of the ~8,000 Sembawang North BTO flats reach MOP around 2034–37 Estimate.
  • A 17–19 min walk to the MRT, which is worse than the Canberra comparables.
  • Flat prices since 2025.
  • Older blocks next door trade at ~$480k–$605k, a floor that pulls the low case down.

3e. EC and private benchmarks

  • Sembawang Road EC land bid: $692 psf ppr Verified. Canberra Drive EC: ~$825 psf ppr Verified. Launch prices are likely in the $1,600s–$1,800s psf Estimate.
  • Parc Life EC resale: ~$1,230–$1,425 psf Unverified.
  • Takeaway: HDB at ~$525–$790 psf versus ECs and condos at ~$1,300–$1,800 psf. The 5-year MOP leaves room to upgrade, but the gap is wide Estimate.

  • Income ceilings: $16,000 for families and $8,000 for singles, for HFE applications from 24 Aug 2026 Official. The extended-family ceiling is reported at $21,000 Unverified.

6. Summary

Pros

  • Fast: keys estimated 1Q 2030 (34-month wait) Official.
  • Standard terms: 5-year MOP, no clawback, open resale market, and rental and private-property purchase allowed after MOP Official.
  • Good value:
    • 4-room $312k–$428k and 5-room $479k–$614k before grants; from $232k and $424k after grants Official.
    • About 31–45% below HDB’s nearby resale comparables Estimate.
  • Good odds for first-timer families: recent first-timer rates for 4-room and 5-room were 0.4–0.6 applicants per flat Official.
  • Lots of large flats: 417 five-room and 464 four-room flats Official.
  • Schools:
    • Northoaks (219 m), Endeavour (499 m) and Canberra Primary (762 m) are within 1 km of every block.
    • Canberra Secondary is 563 m away; preschool on site [Verified/Official].
  • Close by:
    • On-site supermarket and eating house Official.
    • Sembawang Mart (850 m walk), Jelutung Harbour Park (300 m), and the 1-ha park next door Verified.
  • Long-term catalysts: Shipyard waterfront, RTS Link (Feb 2027), North–South Corridor (2029–31) Verified.

Cons

  • Not near an MRT station: the only walkable one is Sembawang, 17–19 min on foot. Expect ~60–65 min door to door to Raffles Place Verified / Estimate.
  • Estate still being built: years of nearby construction, and no date for the Neighbourhood Centre Official.
  • Nursing home site right next to the project Official.
  • MOP supply wave around 2034–37, and resale prices have been flat since 2025 Estimate / Verified.
  • No hospital or large mall within walking distance: Sun Plaza is a 21-min walk and KTPH is ~5.5 km Verified.
  • Competitive for some groups: second-timers (3.4–8.9x) and singles for 2-room Flexi (4–4.7x) Official.
  • Unverified: construction stage and stack orientation.

Who it suits

  • First-timer families who want a 4-room or 5-room flat by ~2030 at a low price.
  • People who work or study in the north (Woodlands, Yishun, JB via the RTS Link), or who drive.
  • Planned upgraders who want the 5-year MOP and the option to rent out the whole flat.
  • Seniors looking at short-lease 2-room Flexi flats.

Less suited to:

  • Daily CBD commuters who rely on the MRT.
  • Buyers looking for strong price growth.
  • Second-timers and singles hoping for easy ballot odds.

Verdict [Estimate/opinion]

A solid value pick for families, not a capital-gains play. You get fast keys, Standard terms, low first-timer competition for large flats, and prices 30–45% below comparable resale flats. Expect healthy equity at MOP but modest growth after that: the location is outer-suburban, 17–19 minutes’ walk from the MRT, and many Sembawang North flats reach MOP at the same time.

Rating: about 7/10 to live in for families; about 5.5/10 for investment upside.

Before applying, check:

  • Each block’s distance to Wellington Primary on OneMap.
  • Which stacks face the nursing-home site or get west sun.
  • The block-level walk to Sembawang MRT.
