HDB's completion and delivery note

Estimated Completion Date (ECD) is the estimated date that the project will be completed. As ECD will depend on the progress of construction works on site, HDB shall not be responsible for any damage or loss arising from a change in the ECD. The ECD of this project is 3Q 2030. Delivery Possession Date (DPD) is the contractual date in the Agreement for Lease by which HDB is required to deliver vacant possession of the booked flat to buyers. DPD is later than the ECD. The estimated DPD for this project is 31 Mar 2032. HDB precincts are planned to serve the evolving needs of the community. There are spaces catered for future inclusion of amenities/ facilities (such as preschools, eldercare facilities, education centres, residents' network centres, day activity centres, etc), commercial facilities (such as shops and eating houses), mechanical and electrical rooms, and such other facilities depending on the prevailing needs, even after residents have moved into the precincts. These spaces could be in the void decks, common property, car parks, or stand-alone community buildings. In addition, housing projects may be located near roads, railways, airbases, construction sites, and land used for non-residential purposes including but not limited to industrial, commercial or utility uses.   • Oasis 4-room is about 13% dearer at both ends of the range.

Site plan Official:

  • Preschool on the ground floor of the 6-storey car park. The car park has a roof garden with adult fitness, a community garden and a shelter.
  • Minimart and shops on the ground floor of block 910B, facing Bayshore Drive.
  • Further facilities and amenities on the ground floor of blocks 910A and 908A, plus space reserved for future community use.
  • Children’s playgrounds, adult and elderly fitness stations, a hardcourt, precinct pavilions, drop-off porches, and roof gardens on 3 residential blocks.
  • An electrical substation and a utility centre on site.
  • Some 4-room flats have full-height windows. Rubbish goes into a pneumatic waste conveyance system. Precinct facilities are open to the public.
  • The estate is car-lite. Bayshore Drive is a bus-only Transit Priority Corridor along a 400 m stretch, with future bus stops beside the site.

Surroundings on the site plan Official:

  • A park directly east, then a drainage reserve and more park.
  • A site reserved for health and medical care to the east.
  • A future elevated linkbridge on the east side, still under study. HDB does not say where it leads.
  • A site for future high-rise housing to the south-west.
  • Public housing under construction to the north-east.
  • Bayshore Sails to the north.

HDB says the Bayshore neighbourhood will have about 3 ha of parks Official. Home & Decor suggests avoiding west-facing stacks (afternoon sun) and low floors near the 910B shops (noise) Unverified.

4. BTO type and stipulations (Plus)

Rule What applies
MOP 10 years Official
Clawback 9% of the resale price or valuation, whichever is higher. Paid by the first owner only; later resale buyers pay none Official
Whole-flat rental Never allowed, even after the MOP. This binds every future owner Official
Room rental Only for 3-room or bigger flats Official. 4-room owners can rent out a room under HDB rules. 2-room Flexi owners cannot rent out rooms
Short-lease 2-room Flexi Cannot be rented out (room or whole flat) or sold on the open market, and has no clawback Official
Private property Can be bought after the MOP Official. Since the flat can’t be rented out, you would have to keep living in it or sell it
Resale buyers: citizenship Same rules as for new flats. A family needs one Singapore Citizen plus at least one other SC or PR Official. propertynet.sg’s claim that PR-only households can buy is wrong
Resale buyers: income Family ceiling $16,000 for 4- and 5-room ($24,000 with extended family), $8,000 for 2-room Flexi Official, HDB couples & families page. HDB’s singles page also shows “Plus flat: $16,000” (read 4 Nov 2026) Official
Resale buyers: other No first-timer requirement. Owners of private property must have sold it 30 months before applying for the eligibility letter (HFE). Buyers who have had two housing subsidies are barred only from resale Prime, not Plus. Resale CPF grants apply Official
New-flat eligibility Family ceiling $16,000 for 4-room and $8,000 for 2-room Flexi. Singles must be 35 or over and can buy only 2-room Flexi, with an $8,000 ceiling. An HFE letter is required Official

6. Summary

Pros

  • 3-minute walk to Bayshore MRT (TE29). Bedok South opens 26 Dec 2026, and Sungei Bedok gives a DTL link 2 stops away. Direct trains to Marina Bay, Shenton Way and Orchard.
  • 4-room at $559k–$786k, against $900k–$1.19m at Bedok South Horizon and HDB’s own comparison of $905k–$985k.
  • 2-room Flexi from $230k. Short-lease flats for seniors from $77k.
  • A 38-month wait, with the contract already awarded.
  • Minimart, shops and preschool on site. A park next door. Car-lite estate on a bus-only street.
  • 855 flats, a smaller project than Sails.

Cons

  • 10-year MOP and a 9% clawback, against 7% at Palms and Vista.
  • No whole-flat rental ever. No room rental for 2-room Flexi.
  • The buyer pool for resale is restricted.
  • No 3-room or 5-room flats.
  • Only one primary school (Temasek Primary) within 1 km, and one more within 2 km.
  • Hawker centres, polyclinic and malls are all 1.3–2.1 km away in a straight line.
  • East Coast Park is about 1.1 km on foot until a linkbridge exists.
  • The estate will be a construction zone for years.
  • About 13% dearer than the 2024 launches.
  • Many Plus flats nearby reach MOP around 2038–41. Long Island is a long-term unknown.

Who it suits

  • Long-term owner-occupiers who want the East Coast and a direct TEL commute.
  • Singles aged 35 or over and seniors looking at 2-room Flexi, accepting that rooms can’t be rented.
  • Not for: investors; anyone planning to upgrade within 10 years; families needing 3- or 5-room; parents set on several primary schools within 1 km.

Verdict Estimate: very good transport and a good coastal location for the price. Financial upside is limited by the 9% clawback and the Plus rules. Apply if you will live there for 10 years or more.
