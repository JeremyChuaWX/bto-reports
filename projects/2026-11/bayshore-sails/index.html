HDB's completion and delivery note

Estimated Completion Date (ECD) is the estimated date that the project will be completed. As ECD will depend on the progress of construction works on site, HDB shall not be responsible for any damage or loss arising from a change in the ECD. The ECD of this project is 1Q 2031. Delivery Possession Date (DPD) is the contractual date in the Agreement for Lease by which HDB is required to deliver vacant possession of the booked flat to buyers. DPD is later than the ECD. The estimated DPD for this project is 30 Sep 2032. HDB precincts are planned to serve the evolving needs of the community. There are spaces catered for future inclusion of amenities/ facilities (such as preschools, eldercare facilities, education centres, residents' network centres, day activity centres, etc), commercial facilities (such as shops and eating houses), mechanical and electrical rooms, and such other facilities depending on the prevailing needs, even after residents have moved into the precincts. These spaces could be in the void decks, common property, car parks, or stand-alone community buildings. In addition, housing projects may be located near roads, railways, airbases, construction sites, and land used for non-residential purposes including but not limited to industrial, commercial or utility uses. Hence, residents may experience related disamenities including but not limited to higher noise levels and smell nuisance.   • The Sails 4-room floor price is about 13–17% above the 2024 Bayshore launches.
  • Its top price is below Oasis’s top price.

After grants

  • Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG):
    • First-timer families earning up to $9,000 a month can get up to $120,000. The amount steps down to $0 at the $9,000 ceiling.
    • First-timer singles can get up to $60,000.
    • [Unverified: not re-checked against HDB for this exercise.]
  • Worked example for a 4-room Estimate:
    • A household that gets the full $120k EHG would pay about $460k–$638k.
    • A household earning above $9,000 a month pays the full $580k–$758k.

Eligibility

  • You need a valid HFE letter. Letters for this exercise had to be applied for by 15 Sep 2026. Verified: mynicehome
  • BTO income ceilings are $16,000 for families and $8,000 for singles buying a 99-year 2-room Flexi. These apply to HFE applications from 24 Aug 2026, and this is the first exercise under them. [Official: HDB Annex A, “Revised Income Ceilings”]
  • Singles aged 35 and above may apply for 2-room Flexi only. Unverified: general framework rule

3c. Plus-specific factors

  1. 10-year MOP Official.
    • Keys are expected from about 1Q 2031, so the earliest sale is about 2041 Estimate.
    • The flat would then have about 89 years of lease left.
  2. 9% clawback on your sale Official.
    • It is charged on the higher of resale price or valuation, so $90k on a $1M sale.
    • Only the first owner pays it.
  3. Restricted resale buyer pool.
    • Resale Plus buyers who take no CPF Housing Grant must still be within an income ceiling: $16,000 for families and $24,000 for extended families (up from $14,000). Singles buying resale Plus without the Singles Grant: $16,000. Official: HDB Annex A
    • Sources still quoting $14,000 are out of date.
    • At least one buyer must be a Singapore Citizen. Unverified
    • Private property owners face a 30-month wait-out before buying. Unverified
  4. No whole-flat rental, ever Official: mynicehome. This removes landlord demand.
  5. Offsetting factors.
    • Later resale buyers pay no clawback.
    • The location is genuinely scarce: MRT at the doorstep, seafront, city-fringe.

No Plus or Prime flat has reached MOP yet, so there is no market evidence of the size of the restriction discount. Commentators suggest 5–15%. Unverified

Construction risk notes Estimate:

  1. Reclaimed ground. Bayshore sits on reclaimed land from the 1970s, so deep piling is needed. This is routine for HDB but adds some schedule risk.
  2. Large project. At 1,695 units, one contractor’s delay affects many households. The DPD gives 18 months of buffer.
  3. Years of nearby works, which will continue after move-in:
    • Vela Bay
    • The integrated MRT/bus interchange site
    • Bayshore Oasis
    • Further housing to the south and east
    • The ECP flyover (to about 2030)
    • Long Island preparatory works offshore from end-2026
  4. Rail risk is now low. TEL5 and DTL3e open 26 Dec 2026, before you collect keys.

6. Summary

Pros

  • MRT at the doorstep. Bayshore TE29 is about a 2-minute walk. Bedok South and the Sungei Bedok TEL–DTL interchange open 26 Dec 2026.
  • Seafront living. East Coast Park is about 460 m away in a straight line, but about 1.2 km on foot today.
  • Low price per square foot. About $599–$782 psf for a 4-room, against about $984 psf for nearby Bedok South Horizon resale and about $2,886 psf for Vela Bay next door.
  • Strong on-site care facilities. Preschool, kidney dialysis centre, Active Ageing Centre, eating house and Residents’ Network Centre, with a nursing home next door.
  • Hospital and polyclinic within reach. Changi General Hospital is about 3 km away and Bedok Polyclinic about 1.8 km.
  • Temasek Primary is likely within 1 km.
  • A planned new estate. Bus-priority corridor, parks with a heritage trail, an integrated MRT/bus hub, and a future SAFRA clubhouse.

Cons

  • Plus restrictions:
    • 10-year MOP, so the earliest exit is about 2041
    • 9% clawback
    • No whole-flat rental ever
    • Resale buyers limited by citizenship and income ceilings
  • A long wait. 44 months, completion 1Q 2031, DPD Sep 2032. This is not a short-wait project.
  • Only 90 three-room flats and no 5-room, so it is not ideal for growing families wanting space.
  • Thin amenities today. The nearest hawker centre and full supermarket are about a 15–20 minute walk, and Bedok Mall is about 1.85 km away.
  • Narrow primary school choice. Only Temasek Primary is within 1 km.
  • Years of surrounding construction and offshore Long Island works.
  • Large Bayshore Plus supply will reach MOP at the same time.
  • Higher prices than the 2024 Bayshore launches.

Who it suits

  • Owner-occupiers who will stay 10+ years, such as couples or small families content with a 4-room, who value East Coast living and TEL/DTL access to the CBD, Changi and Tampines.
  • Singles and seniors (2-room Flexi, including short-lease options), given the on-site eldercare and dialysis facilities.
  • Not for:
    • Buyers wanting a 5-year exit to a condo
    • Buyers wanting rental income from the whole flat
    • Buyers who need a wide choice of primary schools
    • Buyers who need keys before 2031

Verdict Estimate

A strong pick for long-term own-stay, and a weak one as a wealth-building stepping stone. For a 4-room household that wants the East Coast and will stay past 2041, Sails combines doorstep MRT, the seafront and city-fringe access at a large discount to comparable resale and condo prices.

On demand, the October 2024 Bedok exercise, which included the first Bayshore projects, had a 4-room first-timer application rate of about 2.1–2.5, with about 3.9 applications per flat overall across all applicants Unverified: MoneySmart, Stacked Homes. The “about 5×” figure used earlier is not supported. The small 3-room quota will likely be the most competitive.

Compared with Bayshore Oasis Official:

  • Oasis is about two quarters faster, with completion in 3Q 2030.
  • Oasis’s 4-room range is wider, at $559k–$786k.
  • Oasis has no 3-room flats.
  • Both carry a 9% clawback.
