Bottom line. HDB’s upcoming Bishan project (code BH_N3C42A) is a 1,070-unit project on the new Sin Ming Street, off Sin Ming Road and Marymount Road Official. It is almost certainly the Shorter Waiting Time (SWT) project The Straits Times reported in Aug 2026: already under construction and due for completion in mid-2030 Estimate, high confidence. So keys should come in about 3 years rather than the usual 4. It is 2-room Flexi heavy (49% of units), with a supermarket and preschool on site Official. There are no 5-room flats Official. The site is on the edge of the Sin Ming industrial estate, about 0.8 km walk from Upper Thomson (TEL) and Marymount (CCL) MRT stations. It is within 1 km of Catholic High and borderline for Ai Tong. Plus is the most likely classification Estimate. Expect 4-room prices of roughly S$500k–720k before grants if it is Plus Estimate.

1c. Likely classification Estimate

Outcome Probability Reasoning
Plus ~50–55% Central, mature town with a strong school belt. But it is not at an MRT doorstep: two stations are ~0.8 km walk, and the town centre is ~1.1 km straight. PropNex’s Wong Siew Ying (via ST) suggested Plus because of the city-fringe location.
Standard ~25–30% Industrial-fringe site with heavy-vehicle parks next door. ERA’s Eugene Lim (via ST) suggested Standard: quieter, and far from MRT.
Prime ~15–20% Bishan’s two framework-era projects, Bishan Terraces (Oct 2025) and Lakeview Cascadia (Jun 2026), were both Prime Verified. But both are better located: town centre and MacRitchie edge respectively.

The MND framework classifies by proximity to the city, connectivity and surrounding amenities Official. On all three, this site is weaker than Bishan’s Prime precedents.

3e. Capital appreciation: my view Estimate

Supports value

  • Mature Bishan address.
  • Catholic High within 1 km.
  • Three MRT lines within about 1 km, and two CRL stations from 2030.
  • Sub-regional-centre plans: offices, polyclinic, hawker centre.
  • New flats in a pocket of mostly 1970s–90s stock.

Holds it back

  • Industrial and heavy-vehicle neighbours.
  • No MRT within 750 m.
  • No 5-room flats.
  • The 2-room Flexi share (49%) makes the project less family-focused.
  • Sin Ming Residences reaches MOP earlier and competes with it.
  • If Plus or Prime: a 10-year MOP and a clawback.
  • Bishan prices flattened in 2026.

Verdict on value: a sound home with moderate upside. It will trade at a discount to the Bishan St 22/24 and town-centre estates, not at Natura Loft or Bishan Terraces levels.

  • Construction is already underway: HDB builds SWT flats ahead of launch.
  • Comparable waits for Nov 2026 launches Official:

Construction and occupation risks Estimate

  • Industrial neighbours: heavy-vehicle parks and workshops to the north mean dust, noise and lorry traffic. This persists after you move in; it is not just a construction-period issue.
  • North-South Corridor: works along Marymount Rd until its completion (~2029 Unverified).
  • Overlapping construction: Sin Ming Street works, and possibly the adjacent civic/community plot.
  • Generic delay risk: contractor insolvency hit some projects in 2023. HDB has generally delivered on time since.

6. Summary

Pros

  • Short wait: keys in about 3 years (~mid-2030), from a site already being built Estimate.
  • Mature central town:
    • About 0.8 km walk (10–11 min) to two MRT lines: CCL at Marymount, TEL at Upper Thomson Verified.
    • Two Cross Island Line stations about 1.1 km away from 2030 Official.
  • Top schools nearby:
    • Catholic High (Primary) within 1 km Verified.
    • Ai Tong borderline within 1 km Estimate.
    • Whitley Secondary, Eunoia JC and RI are close.
  • Everyday amenities: supermarket and preschool on site Official. FairPrice and Bishan North shops about 0.6–0.8 km walk Verified.
  • Price gap: likely $200k–400k cheaper than newer 4-room resale in Toa Payoh or AMK Estimate.
  • 2-room Flexi supply: 520 units, so a good shot for singles 35+ and seniors who want Bishan Official.

Cons

  • Industrial-fringe site: heavy-vehicle parks, workshops and temples next door. HDB flags industries within 500 m Official.
  • No MRT doorstep: about 0.8 km walk at best. No NEA hawker centre in Bishan; Shunfu Mart is about 0.84 km walk Verified.
  • Nearest polyclinic (Ang Mo Kio) is about 2.6 km walk Verified.
  • No 5-room or 3Gen flats, and only 80 3-room flats Official.
  • If Plus or Prime: 10-year MOP, clawback, never rent out the whole flat, and a $16k income cap on resale buyers Official.
  • Heavy 4-room demand likely: Lakeview drew 3.9× from first-timers and 18.5× from second-timers Official.
  • Unknowns: name, classification and prices are unpublished, and HDB has not confirmed the SWT link.

Who it suits

  • First-timer families who want to be in Bishan soon, especially boys’ families targeting Catholic High.
  • Singles 35+ and seniors who want a central 2-room Flexi.
  • Workers in the CBD, Marina Bay or along the TEL/CCL.

Who it doesn’t suit

  • Buyers sensitive to industrial noise and traffic.
  • Larger households.
  • Anyone who may need to sell or rent out the whole flat within 10 years (if Plus or Prime).
  • Investors.

Verdict Estimate

A good-value, fast-delivery way into Bishan, but not a premium Bishan location. If HDB confirms Plus with 4-room prices around $500k–720k and a wait of about 3 years, it is one of the better Feb 2027 options for owner-occupier families and singles. Visit the Sin Ming Rd/Marymount Rd edge at a weekday peak before applying. In the Feb 2027 brochure, confirm the classification, clawback rate, waiting time and the block’s distance to Ai Tong.
